01
The question

Can a machine do science?

“I build AI that conducts academic research. And because peer review, papers, and universities are historical accidents rather than optimal designs, I am equally drawn to the other question: what new forms should science take when AI becomes a researcher? The design space of science has barely been explored — I want to create the new games of knowledge-making, not just play the old one.”
